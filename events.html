CLEF shared tasks

Touché · Argument retrieval

Panchenko helped organize the first three Touché labs. The comparative-question task asked systems to retrieve arguments that help a reader choose between alternatives.

PAN · Multilingual text detoxification

The Webis-hosted task challenged teams to rewrite toxic text in neutral language while preserving its meaning. The 2025 edition expanded language coverage to include Tatar, Hinglish, Japanese, and others.

SemEval shared tasks

2025 · Task 11 · Multilingual emotion detection

Using the BRIGHTER dataset, teams identified emotions expressed in text across languages. The organizers received the SemEval Best Task Paper Award.

Task overview · Award

2026 · Task 3 · Dimensional aspect-based sentiment analysis

DimABSA asked systems to identify what a speaker expresses an opinion about and model sentiment along two dimensions, valence and arousal.

Task overview

RUSSE at Dialogue Evaluation

The Russian Semantic Evaluation series brings teams together to evaluate systems on a common dataset. Each edition focused on a different problem in Russian.

  • 2015 · Word similarity and relatedness — estimate how closely two words are related.
  • 2018 · Word sense induction and disambiguation — group uses of the same word by meaning.
  • 2020 · Taxonomy enrichment — place new words under suitable parent concepts.
  • 2022 · Text detoxification — rewrite toxic sentences using parallel toxic and neutral examples.

AIST conference

Analysis of Images, Social Networks and Texts

Panchenko chaired the NLP track and helped edit proceedings during 2014–2018. He joined the steering committee in 2019 and served as a head of organization for the series in 2020 and 2021. Later editions he helped organize include Yerevan (2023), Bishkek (2024), Almaty (2025), and Astana (2026).

TextGraphs · Graph-based methods for NLP

Panchenko co-organized TextGraphs-14 (2020), TextGraphs-15 (2021), TextGraphs-16 (2022), and TextGraphs-17 (2024). The 2024 workshop also ran a shared task on combining text and graph representations to answer questions over a knowledge graph.

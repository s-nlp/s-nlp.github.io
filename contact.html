About

Alexander Panchenko is a leading Russian researcher in computational linguistics. He has led the research group since its founding in April 2019.

Research

Panchenko has authored or co-authored more than 200 peer-reviewed publications, including papers at CORE A* conferences and articles in Q1 journals in natural language processing.

Selected awards

2026 · Vladimir Levenshtein Award. Panchenko received the award for building a research group recognized for its productivity in text processing. S-NLP regards it as Russia’s highest distinction in natural language processing. Laureates and award citation

2025 · ACL Best Resource Paper. Panchenko and his co-authors received the award for BRIGHTER, a collection of human-annotated emotion recognition datasets covering 28 languages. ACL awards

2025 · SemEval Best Task Paper. The organizers of Task 11, including Panchenko, received the award for Bridging the Gap in Text-Based Emotion Detection, the multilingual shared task built on BRIGHTER. SemEval awards

2016 · RepL4NLP Best Paper. Maria Pelevina, Nikolay Arefiev, Chris Biemann, and Alexander Panchenko received the award for Making Sense of Word Embeddings at the first Workshop on Representation Learning for NLP at ACL. Read the paper

Career and education

Before leading S-NLP, he worked as a researcher at the University of Hamburg and the Technical University of Darmstadt in Germany.

He graduated from Bauman Moscow State Technical University and earned his PhD in computational linguistics at the Université catholique de Louvain in Belgium. In 2024, he earned his habilitation (Doctor of Computer Science) at HSE University.
